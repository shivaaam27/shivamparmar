# Image prompts for the About cards

Two prompts for Gemini (Imagen) or ChatGPT (image generation). Each prompt is one
block: paste it whole. Ask for a **4:3 landscape** image (the cards crop to 4:3);
if the tool offers sizes, pick the largest. Generate 4 and keep the most natural one.

---

## 1. AI & prompt engineering

A quiet, editorial product photograph of a person's hands working at a minimal desk, shot for a premium technology brand's annual report. Seen from slightly above and to the side, at about forty-five degrees. A thin aluminium laptop sits open on a pale oak desk; its screen shows a clean, dark code editor with a few lines of calm monospaced text and a narrow side panel of chat-style messages, softly out of focus so no text is readable. One hand rests lightly on the trackpad, the other holds a matte black pen above a small open notebook with a few handwritten arrows and boxes sketching a workflow: three rounded boxes joined by lines, drawn in graphite, slightly smudged, clearly by a real hand. A ceramic cup of black coffee with a faint ring stain on the wood, a folded pair of reading glasses, and a single sprig of eucalyptus in a small glass bottle sit at the edge of the frame, partly cut off. Natural window light from the left, late morning, soft and directional, casting long gentle shadows and a subtle highlight along the laptop's edge. The palette is almost monochrome: warm whites, pale greys, light oak, charcoal, with the only saturation coming from the muted green eucalyptus. Shot on a full-frame mirrorless camera with a 50mm lens at f/2.8, ISO 200, shallow depth of field so the notebook and hands are sharp while the screen and background fall off gently; real optical bokeh, a touch of natural lens vignetting, fine film-like grain, true-to-life skin texture with visible pores, fine hairs on the wrist, a slightly uneven fingernail, a small crease in the notebook page, faint dust on the desk in the light. Plenty of empty negative space at the top of the frame for layout. Composition follows the rule of thirds; nothing is centred perfectly. Photographic realism, the look of an unretouched magazine photograph, not a render.

Avoid: no glowing holograms, no floating icons, no neon, no blue sci-fi light, no robot, no brain illustrations, no circuit patterns, no floating code, no visible logos or brand names, no readable screen text, no watermark, no text overlay, no extra fingers, no fused or missing fingers, no distorted hands, no plastic or waxy skin, no over-smoothed skin, no symmetrical perfect composition, no HDR look, no oversaturated colours, no oversharpening, no 3D render, no CGI, no illustration, no cartoon, no stock-photo smile, no face in frame, no cluttered desk, no cables everywhere, no harsh flash, no artificial studio glow.

## 2. Pharmacy

A calm, close-up editorial photograph inside a clean, modern pharmaceutical compounding laboratory, shot for a medical journal's feature story. The frame is tight on a pharmacist's gloved hands grinding a fine white powder with a pestle in a matte white porcelain mortar, placed on a spotless pale grey laboratory bench. The hands wear thin, well-fitted nitrile gloves in a soft pale blue-grey, slightly wrinkled at the knuckles, with the cuff of a crisp white lab coat visible at the wrist, a single faint crease in the fabric. A small cloud of powder dust hangs in the air, catching the light. Around the mortar, arranged naturally rather than perfectly: a stainless steel spatula resting across a folded sheet of glassine weighing paper holding a small heap of white powder; a digital analytical balance with its clear glass draft shield, slightly out of focus; two amber glass bottles with plain white blank labels; a small glass beaker with clear liquid and a measured meniscus; a few empty gelatin capsule halves in a shallow glass dish. Soft, even daylight from large frosted windows behind and to the right, with a clean backlight rim along the pestle and the gloved fingers, and gentle shadows falling toward the camera. The palette is restrained and clinical but warm: whites, pale greys, brushed steel, the honey tone of the amber glass, and the muted blue-grey of the gloves. Shot on a full-frame camera with a 100mm macro lens at f/4, ISO 400, shallow depth of field so the mortar, pestle and powder are crisp while the balance and bottles fall into soft, creamy bokeh; subtle natural vignetting, fine realistic film grain, micro-texture on the porcelain, faint fingerprints and a tiny scratch on the steel spatula, a few stray grains of powder on the bench. Leave calm negative space in the upper part of the frame. Composition off-centre on the rule of thirds. The overall feeling: precision, care and quiet expertise. Photographic realism, the look of an unretouched documentary photograph, not a render.

Avoid: no pharmacy shop or retail shelves, no shop counter, no price tags, no pills spilling dramatically, no colourful candy-like tablets, no syringes, no needles, no blood, no hospital bed, no face in frame, no visible logos or brand names, no readable label text, no watermark, no text overlay, no extra fingers, no fused or missing fingers, no distorted hands, no bare hands where gloves should be, no plastic or waxy look, no over-smoothed textures, no perfect symmetry, no HDR look, no oversaturated colours, no teal-and-orange grade, no oversharpening, no 3D render, no CGI, no illustration, no cartoon, no neon or coloured lab lighting, no smoke effects, no sci-fi laboratory.
